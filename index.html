MANUAL / HOW TO PLAY

PLAY: Tap any of the 16 pads for a sound. Tap quickly to change the attack. Press and hold for rapid fragments. Multiple fingers work at once.

SOUND BANK: SYNTH uses the original 16 synthesized voices. SAMPLE uses 16 automatically chopped variations of your recording or imported audio. Tap the SOUND BANK button to switch. Your original SYNTH kit remains untouched.

MIC: Tap once to start recording, then tap again to stop. Recording stops automatically after 12 seconds. Grant microphone permission when asked. Headphones are recommended to prevent feedback. Microphone recording requires HTTPS or localhost.

FILE: Import an audio recording from your device (WAV, MP3, M4A, or another format supported by your browser). The first 12 seconds are analyzed and arranged across the 16 pads. Files remain on your device. To remove an imported bank, use .

LOOP: Capture recent playing into a two-bar loop. If nothing has been played, a groove is generated. Tap again to stop. New pad hits are added to the loop on the following pass.

MUTATE: Rearrange and transform the current pattern. Hold for a stronger mutation. KEEP: Save a checkpoint. RESTORE: Return to that checkpoint. Hold RESTORE to undo the latest MUTATE/NEW (up to 8 steps).

TAPE: Record your live performance and loop together, for up to 30 seconds. Tap again to render a WAV with a reverb tail. MAKE WAV: Export a seamless two-bar loop as 44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo PCM. Both exports include SAMPLE sounds when that bank is selected.

NEW: Generate a new groove. − / +: Change tempo. Keyboard: 1 2 3 4 / Q W E R / A S D F / Z X C V.

Imported audio is stored locally in this browser when storage is available; it is not uploaded to GitHub. Browser storage can be cleared by the system. Save your original files separately. On iPhone, keep the app open while recording or exporting. Start at a moderate volume.
