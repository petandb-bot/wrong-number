MANUAL / HOW TO PLAY

PADS: Tap to play immediately, tap rapidly for a new attack, or hold for tempo-related repeating fragments. Different pad groups respond with different repeat rhythms. Multiple fingers can play together. No timing skills required.

SOUND KIT: Use the arrows or tap the kit name to switch between CANDY KIT, TOY MALFUNCTION, SUGAR POISON, ROTTEN CARROT and WORM PARADE. The current loop keeps playing; its sounds change immediately on the next notes.

LIVE JAM: Start LOOP and play any pads on top of the beat. Your live performance sounds immediately and does not change the pattern.

● DUB / OVERDUB: Tap DUB to arm recording (and start the loop if stopped). Play pads, then tap DUB to finish. Played notes are snapped to the nearest 16th-note grid, but you hear each pad instantly without quantized delay. Notes start repeating from the following loop pass. DUB works with long-press repeats too. Tap LOOP to stop and save any waiting notes.

LOOP: Capture recent free playing into two bars, or generate a beat if your history is empty. Tap again to stop. When DUB is off, tapping pads never edits the loop.

MUTATE: Evolve the existing pattern with kit-aware changes. Tap MUTATE to produce an audible variation and begin playback if stopped. Hold for a wilder transformation. The LCD shows the new number of notes.

KEEP: Save a pattern and kit snapshot. RESTORE: Recall it. Hold RESTORE to undo previous changes, including overdubs (up to eight states). Changing a pattern while DUB is armed will finish DUB first.

TAPE: Record your playing and the live loop for up to 30 seconds, then save a WAV with a natural tail. WAV: Export an exact two-bar seamless loop as 44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo PCM.

NEW: Generate a new beat using the current kit's rhythmic personality. − / +: Adjust tempo. Desktop keyboard: 1 2 3 4 / Q W E R / A S D F / Z X C V.

NOTE: MIC and FILE have been removed in this version to avoid unstable recording behavior on mobile browsers. Your old patterns still load; previous imported sample banks are not used or erased by this app. All sound is generated locally. Start at a comfortable listening volume.
